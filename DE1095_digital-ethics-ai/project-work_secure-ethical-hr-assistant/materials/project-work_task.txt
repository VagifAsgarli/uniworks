Design a secure and ethical AI system (theoretical)
Choose an imaginary AI system for a real use case, for example an HR assistant, a student-support chatbot or a
code-review agent. You don’t have to build anything. You must describe the system and show how you would make it
secure, ethical and legal. Work must be done individually.
Your presentation must cover:
The system What it does, who uses it, who is affected, and what can go wrong.
Lifecycle For each of the five stages (data, training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring), name one risk and how you would handle
it.
Security At least three threats, which attack types apply (for example prompt injection, data poisoning, model extraction), and one
preventive and one detective control for each. Check your system against the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications.
Governance Who approves what (data, model, human oversight, documentation), and a short incident response plan.
Ethics and law The EU AI Act risk tier and what it requires, data protection, and one real ethical dilemma with your reasoned choice.
Quality and limits How you would measure that it works, and two limits of LLMs that affect your system (for example hallucinations,
outdated knowledge, bias in training data).
7 minutes presentation (online).
