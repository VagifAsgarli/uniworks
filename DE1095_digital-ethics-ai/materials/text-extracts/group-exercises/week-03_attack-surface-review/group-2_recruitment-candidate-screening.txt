Group 2 - AI Recruitment & Candidate Screening
Scenario: alticHire is a large recruitment company that uses an AI
system to screen job applications for its corporate customers.
Applicants upload CVs and cover letters, and the AI compares them with
job requirements. The system uses historical recruitment data and
company hiring criteria to generate a candidate score from 0–100 and
recommend candidates for interview. HR employees review the
recommendations before making the final hiring decision.
