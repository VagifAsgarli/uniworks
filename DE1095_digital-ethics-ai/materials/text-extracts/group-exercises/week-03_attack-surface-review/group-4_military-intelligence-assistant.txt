Group 4 - AI Military Intelligence Assistant
Scenario: A NATO member state's defence organisation uses an internal AI
assistant to help analysts process large amounts of intelligence information. The
system summarises reports, searches classified and unclassified document
repositories and helps analysts identify relationships between events. Employees
authenticate using organisational accounts, and access to information depends
on their security clearance. Analysts use the AI's outputs to support their work,
but final intelligence assessments are made by humans.
