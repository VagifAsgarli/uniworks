Group 3 - AI Banking Customer Assistant
Scenario: NorthBank has introduced an AI assistant into its online and mobile
banking services. Customers can ask questions about banking products and,
after authentication, ask about their accounts and recent transactions. The
assistant searches an internal bank knowledge base and can access customer
information. It can also create support cases and initiate certain customer-​
service requests, although sensitive transactions require additional
confirmation.
