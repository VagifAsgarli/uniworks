Course Goals
2
To provide a practical introduction to the field of Sociotechnical Systems Modelling;
To learn systems analysis and design step-by-step, starting with gathering and concretizing ideas and continuing with quantitative modelling;
To introduce with different modelling and simulation technologies, which serve for conceptualization, modelling dynamic changes in the state of systems and for analysis of individual events.
