# Yellowstone stocks-and-flows model: specification

Generated by `tools/build_stmx.py` from `tools/yellowstone_model.py`; the STELLA file has the same equations. This is what the Equation layer of STELLA lists.

Run specification: from 1995 to 2045, DT = 0.25 years, Euler's method, stocks and flows non-negative.

## Stocks

| Stock | Initial value | Inflows | Outflows | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wolves | `Wolves_released` | wolf births | wolf deaths | wolves | Wolves living in the park. The run starts in 1995 with the wolves that were released. |
| Deers | `Initial_deers` | deer births | deer deaths, predation | deers | Deers (elk) of the northern Yellowstone herd, the main prey of the wolves. |
| Vegetation | `Initial_vegetation` | regrowth | browsing | percent | Condition of the willow, aspen and cottonwood stands, in percent of what the land can carry (100 = full). |
| Beavers | `Initial_beaver_colonies` | colony growth | colony loss | colonies | Beaver colonies on the streams of the northern range. |

Each stock follows `Stock(t) = Stock(t - dt) + (inflows - outflows) * dt`.

## Flows

| Flow | Equation | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| wolf births | `Wolves_born_per_deer_eaten*predation` | wolves/year | Pups that survive their first year: the more deers the packs kill, the more pups they raise. |
| wolf deaths | `Wolves*(Wolf_death_rate+Fights_and_diseases+Wolf_hunting_rate)` | wolves/year | Wolves dying of age, of fights between packs and diseases (more when the wolves are many) and shot by people. |
| deer births | `Deer_birth_rate*Deers*MAX(0, 1-Deers/Deer_capacity)` | deers/year | Calves that survive: fewer when the herd is close to the number the land can feed. |
| deer deaths | `Deers*(Deer_death_rate*Winter_severity+Bears_and_cougars_rate+Deer_hunting_rate)` | deers/year | Deers dying of age and hunger in winter, killed by bears and cougars, and shot by hunters. |
| predation | `Kill_coefficient*Wolves*Deers` | deers/year | Deers killed by wolves in a year. |
| regrowth | `Vegetation_growth_rate*Vegetation*MAX(0, 1-Vegetation/100)*Beaver_ponds_effect` | percent/year | New growth of willow and aspen: slows down near 100 percent, faster where beaver ponds raise the water table. |
| browsing | `Browsing_per_1000_deers*Vegetation*Deers/1000` | percent/year | Young willow and aspen eaten by the deers. |
| colony growth | `Beaver_growth_rate*Beavers*Free_habitat` | colonies/year | New beaver colonies: beavers settle where tall willow gives food and dam material and the stream is free. |
| colony loss | `Beavers*Beaver_loss_rate` | colonies/year | Colonies abandoned in a year. |
